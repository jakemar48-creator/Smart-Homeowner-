# Start Funnel Override

## Direction

Use a contractor-native, editorial conversion style: strong black typography, signal red for the single primary action, and high contrast white surfaces. This is inspired by the energy and clarity of ChopShop Media's site, not a copy of its brand or layout.

## Tokens

- Display: `Barlow Condensed` 700–800, uppercase only for labels and short headlines.
- Body: `DM Sans` 400–700.
- Ink: `#171717`.
- Accent: `#C7352B`.
- Paper: `#F6F4F0`.
- Success: `#286E4D`.

## Funnel rules

- Preserve the single-question flow, progress, and back navigation.
- Keep ZIP first and provide availability feedback immediately.
- Show only real proof. Do not imitate third-party review badges, contractor claims, or logos.
- The red CTA remains the only primary action per screen.
